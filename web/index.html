Your lectures, organised by course and week. Recordings from the same week go into the same notebook.

  
How NotebookLM works
Sign in through your default browser. voiceNotebook uses the selected account’s local session; no extra browser is installed. macOS may ask for browser control or Keychain access. Safari’s protected session can require Full Disk Access. The sign-in window closes after connecting; your other windows stay open. An unofficial NotebookLM client creates notebooks and uploads sources. Google may occasionally require you to sign in again.
Audio is converted to MP3 and split below 200 MB when needed. Class photos are sent only after your approval.

Voice MemosChecking…

      
To find new Voice Memos automatically, voiceNotebook needs Full Disk Access to read Apple’s protected recordings folder. macOS has no separate Voice Memos permission. Originals are never changed or deleted.

      
Enable voiceNotebook in Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access, then quit and reopen the app. If it is missing, use + to add ~/Applications/voiceNotebook.app.

    

    
Prefer not to grant Full Disk Access? Export recordings to a regular folder and select it below.

    

      
      
      

      
An exported-audio folder works without Full Disk Access. Entering the protected Voice Memos folder path still requires permission. Export new recordings to your chosen folder to include them.
